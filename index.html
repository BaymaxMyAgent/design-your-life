Tony Robbins has used this framework to coach world leaders, Olympic athletes, and top CEOs. In the next 15 minutes, you will use it to build your personal plan.
Tony Robbins — On RPM
R
Results
What do you want — really? Not a vague direction. A specific, compelling, measurable result that your brain can lock onto like a target.
P
Purpose
Why does it matter? Purpose is stronger than the object. When your why is strong enough, the how becomes irrelevant — you find a way.
M
Massive Action Plan
What will you do? Not a to-do list — a real plan, built around your result and fueled by your purpose. Massive action is the cure-all.
What You Are About to Experience
  • Tony's voice will guide you into each question — listen before you write
  • Three phases — Results, Purpose, Massive Action — each building on the last
  • At the end, your personal MAP is generated — specific to exactly what you wrote
  • The more honest you are, the more powerful your MAP will be
Takes about 15 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers.
Just write what is true for you right now.

RESULTS — Question 1
TONY ROBBINS AUDIO PLAYING
What is the ONE thing pulling you right now?
Right now — if everything else fell away — what is the ONE outcome that, if you achieved it, would change everything else?
Don't edit yourself. Don't be practical yet. Just write what's true.
